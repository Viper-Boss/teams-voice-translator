# Bundled background recordings

All bundled recordings are used under CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ .
Files were converted to mono 24 kHz PCM16, trimmed to at most 60 seconds; runtime applies level control and a loop crossfade. Original recordings remain unchanged at the sources.

- room.wav: TheShopCollection_convenience_store_drinks_fridge_drone.wav, LEGIT Audio, The Shop free pack. Actual shop refrigeration/fan ambience, not a library recording. https://opengameart.org/content/the-shop . Only the free OGA samples are CC0.
- rain.wav: Rain on Window Loop, alxl. https://opengameart.org/content/rain-on-window-loop . Author explicitly offers CC0 in attribution notice.
- office.wav: Busy Office No People Loop, Fupicat. https://freesound.org/people/Fupicat/sounds/534123/ . Composition of recorded office sounds (typing, printing and phones), CC0. Public HQ MP3 rendition converted to PCM.
- cafe.wav: coffee shop ambience, waweee. Actual Czech cafeteria recording. https://freesound.org/people/waweee/sounds/370973/ . CC0. Public HQ MP3 rendition converted to PCM; contains background conversations, not speech-free.

No recording here represents a train, airport, library or bedroom. Those earlier synthetic scene labels were removed from the selectable list.
